# Credits

Every sound in DM Sounds is free and openly licensed. CC0 and public-domain recordings need no attribution; the CC BY ones are credited here as their licence asks. All files were trimmed, loudness-matched to -20 LUFS, crossfaded into seamless loops where appropriate, and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

## Collections that made this easy

- [funcoder/omarchy-ambient](https://github.com/funcoder/omarchy-ambient): eight CC0 Freesound field recordings, already cut and loudness-matched.
- [RinvalAi/brown-noise-library](https://github.com/RinvalAi/brown-noise-library): CC0 and public-domain loops from Wikimedia Commons.
- [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org): the bulk of the places, dungeon, eerie, combat, music and stinger sounds.
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org): thunder, bells, gulls, wolves and the campfire.
